Privacy Policy

Al Muadhin
Last updated: 2026-10-28

Al Muadhin ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Al Muadhin mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using Al Muadhin, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Location Data

When you use the prayer times or Qibla feature, the App may request access to your device location. This data is used solely to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction.

We do not send location data to any server or share it with any third party.

b) Notification Permission

The App requests notification permission to alert you about prayer times. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

We do not use any external push notification service.

c) Audio

Adhan sounds are stored locally on your device and work without an internet connection.

We do not record your voice or upload any audio files.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Calculate prayer times and Qibla direction
  • Schedule Adhan reminders
  • Save your preferred settings

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

Your settings are stored locally on your device only. We do not store personal data on any server. Uninstalling the app removes all data.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not include any third-party services such as analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

The App contains no Firebase or any external SDK for data collection.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent

You can contact us for any privacy-related requests.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time.

We will notify users by updating the "Last updated" date above.
